# Roomly tenant prototype

Open `index.html` for the dashboard or `payments.html` for payment history. No installation, internet access, or server is needed. The sibling Home and Login folders provide the linked destinations.

## Included

- Responsive tenant dashboard using the Home prototype's indigo, blue, lavender, typography and rounded elements.
- November bill, confirmed payment, outstanding balance, and a separate pending submission notice.
- Stacked monthly charges chart with a 3/6 month selector, accessible chart data, and a rent/utility donut.
- Six month confirmed payment graph and searchable payment history with status/month filters and reset.
- Payment detail dialogs, current bill dialog, and CSV export of the currently filtered records.

## Sample data

Snapshot date is 25 November 2026. November charges are 5,800 sample units, confirmed funds are 3,000, and outstanding is 2,800. A pending 2,800 submission does not reduce that balance. The due date is 30 November, so the November bill is partially paid, not overdue. June–October bills are fully paid. The total confirmed payment amount across the six months is 31,510. All figures use neutral currency units because a currency has not been selected.

These are fictional frontend records. No authentication, data persistence, payment collection, actual receipt generation, or landlord approval is connected. Opening these files does not enforce real tenant permissions. Payment records and CSV downloads are demonstrations only.
