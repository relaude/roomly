A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you
settle in.

A little reassurance goes a long way.

Do I need to create a new password?

No. Use your existing Google or Facebook account. Roomly’s planned sign-in lets that provider verify your identity without sharing your password with the rental website.

Why does my account need approval?

Sign-in confirms who you are. Your landlord then verifies your account and connects it to your specific tenancy, so you get the right rental records.

Can other tenants see my information?

The planned portal limits tenant access to their own profile and approved tenancy. Your landlord can access the records needed to manage the rental.

Does submitting proof mark my bill as paid?

No. Your landlord must verify that the payment was received. Pending submissions do not reduce your outstanding balance.

Is this a live rental portal?

This is an interactive prototype with sample data. The linked login page demonstrates account states; real Google/Facebook login and tenant records are not connected yet.
